# KERMS #OOTD 👗✨

### *Your closet. Your inspiration. Your OOTD — every day.*

**KERMS #OOTD** is an AI-powered outfit-of-the-day styling chatbot designed to help users decide what to wear based on **their own wardrobe, the occasion, and Pinterest-inspired looks**.

Instead of endlessly scrolling through inspiration and wondering *"But what can I actually wear?"*, #OOTD connects inspiration with the clothes you already own.

---

## 💖 What is #OOTD?

**#OOTD** stands for **Outfit Of The Day**.

The idea is simple: tell the chatbot where you're going, what you're feeling, or what kind of look you want — and #OOTD helps you create an outfit using your personal digital closet.

Whether you're getting ready for:

* 💼 A corporate meeting
* 🤠 A cowboy-themed event
* 💕 Date night
* 💃 A party
* 💍 A wedding
* ☀️ Day wear
* 🌙 Evening wear
* 🎓 A graduation
* ✈️ A holiday
* 🎉 A special event

#OOTD can help you put together a look.

---

## ✨ Core Features

### 👚 1. Digital Closet

Users can build their own digital wardrobe by adding:

* Clothes
* Shoes
* Bags
* Jewelry
* Accessories

Items can be added in two ways:

**🔗 Store Link**

Users can paste a direct link to an item they purchased online.

Instead of keeping the URL as plain text, #OOTD saves the item's **image and relevant information** so it becomes a visual part of the user's digital closet.

**📸 Photo Upload**

Users can also photograph their clothing items and upload the pictures directly.

The goal is to create a visual wardrobe that feels like a personal fashion collection.

---

### 📌 2. Pinterest Collaboration

Pinterest inspiration is a major part of the #OOTD experience.

Users can search for an aesthetic, outfit or event directly through the chatbot.

For example:

> **"Cowboy event"**

#OOTD can immediately surface relevant Pinterest-inspired looks while considering the items already available in the user's closet.

The goal isn't simply to show the user an outfit they like.

It's to help answer:

> **"How can I recreate this vibe using what I already own?"**

---

### 👠 3. Occasion-Based Styling

Users can tell #OOTD what they are dressing for.

Examples:

```text
"I have a corporate meeting tomorrow."

"Help me dress for a cowboy-themed event."

"I need a cute date-night outfit."

"I want something casual but stylish for brunch."
```

The chatbot considers the occasion when generating outfit combinations.

Users can also ask #OOTD to consider or exclude particular items.

---

### 🗂️ 4. Outfit History

#OOTD keeps track of outfits that users have already worn.

Outfits are organized according to categories such as:

* Corporate
* Events
* Day Wear
* Evening Wear
* Casual
* Date Night
* Special Occasions

For example, if a user searches for **Corporate** outfits multiple times, those outfits remain grouped under the same category instead of creating a completely new history every time.

This creates a personal fashion archive over time.

---

### 🔄 5. Repeat Outfit & Cost-per-Wear Tracking

#OOTD can also keep track of how frequently individual items are worn.

This can help users:

* Avoid accidentally repeating the same outfit
* Rediscover clothes they haven't worn recently
* See which items are getting the most use
* Understand the value they're getting from their wardrobe

For example:

> **"You haven't worn this blazer in 3 months 👀"**

or:

> **"You've worn these shoes 12 times!"**

This turns #OOTD into more than an outfit generator — it becomes a tool for managing a personal wardrobe.

## 📱 User Interface

### 🔐 User Login

Users must be able to create an account and log in.

This allows their:

* Digital closet
* Outfit history
* Saved outfits
* Preferences
* Occasion categories

to remain connected to their personal account.

---

### ☰ Collapsible Sidebar

The application includes a sidebar that can collapse to give the chatbot more screen space.

The sidebar can contain sections such as:

```text
🏠 Home
👗 My Closet
✨ Outfit Generator
📌 Pinterest Inspiration
🗂️ Outfit History
❤️ Saved Looks
📊 Wear History
⚙️ Settings
```

When collapsed, the interface becomes cleaner and allows the user to focus on the chatbot.

---

## 💬 The #OOTD Welcome Experience

When users open the chatbot, they should immediately be greeted with a trendy, fashion-forward phrase.

For example:

> **"What's the vibe today? Let's get you dressed. ✨"**

The chatbot should feel like a stylish friend helping the user decide what to wear — not a generic AI assistant.

## 🧠 Example User Flow

### Step 1 — Sign Up

The user creates an account and logs in.

### Step 2 — Build Your Closet

The user uploads clothing items through:

* Store links
* Photos

### Step 3 — Choose Your Vibe

The user tells #OOTD what they need.

Example:

> *"I have a cowboy-themed event this Saturday."*

### Step 4 — Get Inspiration

Pinterest inspiration appears based on the search.

### Step 5 — Style From Your Closet

#OOTD looks at the user's existing items and creates outfit combinations inspired by the requested aesthetic.

### Step 6 — Save or Wear

The user can save the outfit or mark it as **Worn**.

### Step 7 — Build Outfit History

The outfit is automatically added to the appropriate history category.
